# GTM Skill Templates

Pre-built templates for common GTM (Go-To-Market) skill categories.

## Sales Enablement

```yaml
name: handling-objections
description: Generates context-specific objection handling responses for sales calls. Use when preparing for prospect calls, during live objection scenarios, or when training new SDRs on objection handling.
```

Key sections: Common objections by persona, response frameworks (feel-felt-found, acknowledge-bridge-close), competitive positioning.

## Lead Scoring

```yaml
name: scoring-leads
description: Evaluates and scores inbound leads against ICP criteria and engagement signals. Use when qualifying new MQLs, prioritizing outbound targets, or reviewing lead scoring model effectiveness.
```

Key sections: ICP criteria matrix, BANT/MEDDIC frameworks, engagement scoring weights, handoff thresholds.

## Pipeline Management

```yaml
name: reviewing-pipeline
description: Analyzes sales pipeline health, identifies at-risk deals, and generates forecasting insights. Use during pipeline reviews, forecast calls, or when assessing quarter-end projections.
```

Key sections: Stage definitions, velocity metrics, risk signals, forecast methodology.

## Campaign Planning

```yaml
name: planning-campaigns
description: Creates structured marketing campaign briefs with audience targeting, channel strategy, and success metrics. Use when launching new campaigns, planning quarterly marketing programs, or building ABM plays.
```

Key sections: Campaign brief template, audience segmentation, channel mix, budget allocation, KPI targets.

## Competitive Intelligence

```yaml
name: tracking-competitors
description: Synthesizes competitive intelligence from multiple sources into actionable battle cards and positioning guides. Use when updating competitive materials, preparing for competitive deals, or analyzing market movements.
```

Key sections: Competitor profiles, feature comparison, win/loss patterns, talk tracks.

## Customer Success

```yaml
name: managing-customer-health
description: Monitors customer health scores, flags churn risks, and generates QBR preparation materials. Use during customer health reviews, renewal preparation, or expansion opportunity identification.
```

Key sections: Health score components, risk indicators, QBR template, expansion signals.

## Content Creation

```yaml
name: creating-gtm-content
description: Transforms interview transcripts and research into polished GTM content including blog posts, case studies, and whitepapers. Use when converting raw interviews into published content or creating marketing collateral from customer stories.
```

Key sections: Content brief, interview extraction template, narrative structure, CTA framework.

## ABM (Account-Based Marketing)

```yaml
name: researching-accounts
description: Conducts deep account research and generates personalized outreach strategies for target accounts. Use when building ABM account plans, preparing for executive meetings, or personalizing multi-channel outreach sequences.
```

Key sections: Account profile template, stakeholder mapping, personalization variables, engagement sequence.
